沙县小吃进驻中东

Shaxian Snacks Enters Middle East Market with Saudi Flagship Store

中文原文

www.fjsen.com 2025-05-13 09:13:19 罗昱伦 来源：东南网

5月11日，沙县小吃沙特阿拉伯旗舰店在利雅得国际会展中心开业，成为首家在中东开张的沙县小吃加盟连锁店，标志着这一“国民小吃”正式进军中东市场。

开业当天，该店铺的营业额便突破5万元人民币，多款手工现做的传统沙县小吃全部售罄。

“刚尝了一口拌面，和国内的味道一模一样！”来自江苏的周巍已在沙特工作13年，听说沙县小吃阿拉伯旗舰店开业，一大早就赶了过来。“在异国他乡吃到正宗的祖国美食，缓解了我的思乡之情，希望以后能有更多中国美食来到沙特。”

作为中餐美食的代表之一，近年来，沙县小吃借助共建“一带一路”契机积极“出海”。一方面，依托“沙县小吃及图形”集体商标，已先后在日本、美国等77个国家和地区完成商标注册申请工作，并在多国落地；另一方面，加大海外小吃人才培养储备，已举办沙县小吃海外培训班15期，为法国、日本、印度尼西亚等多国合作公司培训技术骨干。

English translation

May 13, 2025, 09:13:19 / By Luo Yulun / Source: FJSEN.com

Shaxian Snacks is a well-known daily dining brand from Shaxian County, Fujian Province, China, offering affordable home-style foods like tossed noodles and steamed dumplings. On May 11, its first franchised flagship store in the Middle East opened at the Riyadh International Convention and Exhibition Center, marking the official entry of this “staple of Chinese daily meals” into the Middle Eastern market.​

On opening day, the store’s revenue exceeded 50,000 RMB, and nearly all freshly handmade traditional Shaxian snacks sold out.

“I just tried the tossed noodles – they taste exactly the same as back home!” said Zhou Wei, a Chinese national from Jiangsu who has worked in Saudi Arabia for 13 years. He arrived early as soon as he heard the news.
“Having authentic Chinese food in a foreign country eases my homesickness. I hope more Chinese cuisines will come to Saudi Arabia.”

As a representative of popular Chinese casual food, Shaxian Snacks has actively expanded overseas in recent years by seizing opportunities under the Belt and Road Initiative.
On one hand, it has completed trademark registration applications for the collective trademark “Shaxian Snacks & Logo” in 77 countries and regions including Japan and the United States, and opened physical stores in many of them.
On the other hand, it has strengthened training and reserve of overseas talents, holding 15 overseas training programs to cultivate technical professionals for cooperative enterprises in France, Japan, Indonesia and other countries.
