平潭购物离境退税实现“即买即退”

Pingtan Introduces On-the-Spot Tax Refund for Tourists

中文原文

2025-05-06 10:35:11 作者：张哲昊 来源：福建日报 责任编辑：周冬

近日，在位于平潭台创园的小购离境退税商店，台胞何奕兴现场购买了一台总价为2299元的手机。随后，他在工作人员的引导下成功办理了退税。

“我过几天就要回台湾了，准备买部手机给家人，在离境退税商店买单后就立刻收到了退税款，没想到这么方便。”何奕兴点赞道。

记者走进小购离境退税商店，只见由中英双语印制的“‘即买即退’商店”标识十分醒目，工作人员正在热情接待旅客。

“境外旅客可在线上通过‘台陆通’旗下的‘黑琵购’平台挑选手机、平板电脑、笔记本电脑以及户外用品等上千种产品，并选择提货点，只要符合‘即买即退’的条件并提供相关证件，即可在提货点支付并办理退税。”商店负责人、福建新通途信息技术有限公司副总经理卫元鹏说。

据国家税务总局平潭综合实验区税务局副局长冯德福介绍，“即买即退”作为离境退税的优化服务举措，其最大的亮点在于将离境口岸退税环节“前移”至购物现场，极大提升了退税效率、通关速度和消费体验，吸引更多境外旅客“买买买”，进一步激活境外旅客再消费的热情，助推平潭“一岛两窗三区”建设。

今年以来，国家大力优化离境退税政策，不断扩大入境消费。在此背景下，国家税务总局平潭综合实验区税务局联合实验区经济发展局、平潭海关等部门建立政税关银联动机制，共同梳理相关业务流程，辅导离境退税商店完成系统升级和设备调试，在“即买即退”服务措施推广当月即开出福建省首单，帮助境外旅客第一时间享受政策红利。

English translation

May 06, 2025, 10:35:11 | By Zhang Zhehao | Source: Fujian Daily | Responsible Editor: Zhou Dong

In a recent visit to the Xiaogou Tax Refund Store in Pingtan Taiwan Innovation Park, Mr. He Yixing, a visitor from Taiwan region of China, purchased a smartphone priced at 2,299 yuan. With assistance from store staff, he successfully claimed a tax refund immediately after payment.

“I’m returning to Taiwan in a few days and wanted to buy a phone for my family. It’s amazing that I received the tax refund right after paying at the store. It couldn’t be more convenient,” he praised.

Inside the store, bilingual signs reading “on-the-spot Tax Refund Available” are prominently displayed, as staff members warmly assist customers.

“International visitors can browse thousands of products, including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and outdoor gear, on the HappyGo platform under the TaiLuTong app, a Cross-Strait digital service platform,” explained Wei Yuanpeng, Deputy General Manager of Fujian Xintongtu Information Technology Co., Ltd., which operates the store. “After selecting a pickup location and providing required documents, eligible travelers can complete payment and tax refund procedures simultaneously at the pickup point.”

According to Feng Defu, Deputy Director of the Pingtan Comprehensive Experimental Zone Tax Service, the “on-the-spot tax refund” represents a major upgrade to the traditional departure tax refund system. By shifting the refund process from the airport to the point of purchase, this initiative significantly enhances efficiency, reduces customs clearance time, and improves the overall shopping experience. " This measure encourages more international tourists to spend in Pingtan and re-engage in post-tax-refund consumption, supporting the development of Pingtan as a Cross-Strait cooperation hub, an opening-up window to the world, and a pilot zone for integrated reform.

This year, China has vigorously optimized the departure tax refund policy to continuously boost inbound consumption. Against this backdrop, the Tax Service of Pingtan Comprehensive Experimental Zone, together with the Zone’s Economic Development Bureau, Pingtan Customs and other departments, established a government-tax-customs-bank linkage mechanism. They jointly sorted out relevant business processes and guided tax refund stores to complete system upgrades and equipment debugging. In the same month that the “on-the-spot tax refund” service was launched, Fujian’s first such tax refund transaction was completed, allowing international travelers to enjoy the policy benefits immediately.
